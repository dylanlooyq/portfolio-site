CSAT & Experience dashboard

One view of how users rate five areas of a service team, benchmarked against an OKR, with the "so what" written for you.

4.87Overall CSAT
101.4%of the 4.8 OKR
5service categories
5survey cycles of trend

The problem

Survey scores alone don't say what to fix. Stakeholders needed to see which category was slipping, why, and what to do next, without reading hundreds of free-text comments.

What I built

  • An overall CSAT scorecard tracked against the OKR.
  • A sentiment breakdown and trend line per category, with the target drawn in.
  • An Automated Insights panel that turns the numbers into Observation → Trend → Action to take.
  • Ranked positive and negative themes per category, with counts, so the driver behind each score is visible.

What it surfaces

Product Relevancy / Design is the weak spot (4.46 and falling), driven by outdated playbooks, while Knowledge Management is climbing (4.86 → 4.97). The dashboard points straight at the fix.
